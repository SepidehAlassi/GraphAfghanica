A CONNECTED VISUAL ARCHIVE

01 / THE PROJECT

Beyond the
individual image.

Photographs are more than visual records. They carry information about people, places, events, and the histories of the collections that preserve them.

GraphAfghanica develops a federated, provenance-aware approach to connecting distributed photographic archives using knowledge graphs, Linked Open Data, and AI-assisted methods — making them easier to discover, contextualize, and study.
