ABOUT / GRAPHAFGHANICA

Images hold stories.
Connections reveal them.

GraphAfghanica is a Digital Humanities initiative connecting historical photographic collections from Afghanistan through interoperable, provenance-aware knowledge graphs.

HISTORICAL PHOTOGRAPHIC HERITAGE · A CONNECTED APPROACH

01 / THE PROJECT

Beyond the archive.

Historical photographs offer invaluable perspectives on Afghanistan’s social, cultural, and urban histories. Yet images are often dispersed across institutions and described in different formats, making them difficult to discover and study together.

GraphAfghanica develops a federated research infrastructure that connects photographic collections without erasing their distinct identities or custodianship. Using Linked Open Data, semantic models, and knowledge graphs, the project links photographs to places, periods, subjects, and sources.

AI-assisted methods can enrich image descriptions and support discovery, while provenance and confidence information help researchers distinguish source metadata from computational interpretations.

02 / RESEARCH OBJECTIVES

What we aim to enable.

01

Connect collections

Make dispersed photographic holdings discoverable through shared semantic descriptions and links.

02

Preserve provenance

Keep source attribution, collection context, and uncertainty visible alongside the images.

03

Explore new questions

Support cross-collection exploration of places, people, themes, and historical change.
